Well, it’s a creative production studio. But really, it’s the creative synthesis of director duo Stuart Bury and Isaiah Powers. From the first spark of an idea to the final frame, they’re there every step of the way, guiding the creative with one goal in mind: crafting a great story, no matter the medium.

Our work has been called creative, quirky, and a little unexpected. We’ll happily take all three. After all, that’s just who we are.

### Isaiah Powers

Isaiah is a problem solver, storyteller, and creative rule-breaker. With a background in film, video, radio, and high-end post-production, he believes no two stories should be told the same way. After years behind the camera and in the edit bay, he discovered that some stories needed more than a camera to fully come alive. So he turned to animation.

As a director and producer, Isaiah pushes visual boundaries, explores tactile techniques, and brings a hands-on approach to every project. His experience in healthcare, shaped by a deeply personal family story, also informs his work in medical and pharmaceutical animation—where clarity, creativity, and meaningful storytelling come together.

### Stuart Bury

Stuart is an award-winning director who believes the most powerful stories are told visually. Like many creative types, he spent his childhood drawing, painting and sculpting. But it wasn’t until he serendipitously stumbled into animation that he knew he’d found his calling.

From character designs to production schedules, Stuart sees every act as a creative one and organizes his creative belief system around the idea that in order to break the rules, you must master them first. Using his diverse creative disciplines, he works with clients and collaborators to break the rules in all the right ways — marrying medium and message, and maximizing impact.

When he’s not working in the studio or on studio projects, he’s spending time with his family and friends and filling up his sketchbook.
