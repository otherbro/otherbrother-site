Google needed to grab their live and streaming audience's attention for an important keynote at their flagship developer's conference, Google I/O.

We worked closely with their team to develop this retro throwback to the first two player game developed, Tennis for Two. The crowd loved the nostalgia, and most importantly focused their attention to hear what was coming next from Google.
